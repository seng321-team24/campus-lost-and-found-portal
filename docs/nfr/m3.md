## 2. NFR-M3-01 Performance

- **Requirement:** For `GET /items` with any combination of `q`, `categoryId`, `dateFrom`, `dateTo`, `location`, `status` and `type`, the 95th percentile response time shall be below 2 seconds on a database with 1,000 listings, with 20 concurrent virtual users. Error rate shall be below 1 %.
- **Measurement method:** Load test of 5 minutes with fictional sample data (1,000 listings, no real personal data). The p95 value and the error rate are read from the report of the load-test tool.
- **How it will be tested:**
  1. Seed 1,000 fictional listings with a script stored in `tests/performance/`.
  2. Run the scenario: 70 % keyword searches, 20 % category and date filters, 10 % detail views.
  3. Store the tool report in `docs/nfr/evidence/` and link it from the summary table.
- **Pass criterion:** p95 < 2 s and errors < 1 % in the report.

## 3. NFR-M3-02 Privacy (KVKK)

- **Requirement:** Responses of `GET /items` and `GET /items/{itemId}` and all M3 screens shall not contain the name, e-mail address or phone number of any user, whether the viewer is a visitor, a logged-in user or the listing owner. The listing owner is identified only by an opaque numeric id.
- **Measurement method:** Count of occurrences of personal data in responses and screens. Target value: 0.
- **How it will be tested:**
  1. Automated API test over 100 seeded listings: assert that no response body contains the fields `fullName`, `email` or `phone` and no string that matches an e-mail pattern.
  2. Manual review of the four M3 screens and the notification e-mail template against the list of personal data fields.
  3. Notification e-mails are checked in the same way (they contain the listing title and status only).
- **Pass criterion:** 0 occurrences in both checks.

## 4. NFR-M3-03 Compatibility

- **Requirement:** The list, search and filter, and detail screens shall be usable on mobile browsers: no horizontal scrolling at 360 × 640 px, and every tap target at least 44 × 44 px.
- **Measurement method:** Viewport check, tap-target check, Lighthouse mobile audit (accessibility score).
- **How it will be tested:**
  1. Open each M3 screen in Chrome on Android and Safari on iOS, and in browser device emulation at 360 × 640 px.
  2. Check that no horizontal scrollbar appears and that filters, pagination and the claim button can be used with one thumb.
  3. Run a Lighthouse mobile audit on the three screens and store the report.
- **Pass criterion:** No horizontal scrolling, all tap targets at least 44 × 44 px, Lighthouse accessibility score at least 90.

## 5. NFR-M3-04 Reliability of notifications

- **Requirement:** At least 95 % of notification e-mails shall be handed to the mail provider within 60 seconds after the triggering event. A failed send shall be retried up to 3 times. A failed e-mail shall never fail or roll back the triggering action.
- **Measurement method:** Time between the event (claim decision or status change) and the provider hand-over, taken from application logs; count of triggering actions that failed because of e-mail errors.
- **How it will be tested:**
  1. Run the application against a local mail sandbox (a test SMTP server that stores messages).
  2. Trigger 20 events (10 claim decisions, 10 status changes) and compare log timestamps.
  3. Stop the sandbox, trigger 5 more events, and check that the actions succeed and that 3 retries are logged per message.
- **Pass criterion:** At least 19 of 20 e-mails within 60 s, and 5 of 5 actions succeed while the provider is down.
